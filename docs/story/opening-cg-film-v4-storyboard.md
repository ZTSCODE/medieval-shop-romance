## 3. Still to confirm

- Details added to the world: the Empire's heartland behind a wall still has harvests; the last town was stormed and its defenders hanged; the Empire has no named founder or ruler in the film; the Empire takes every town's seed corn and breaks its hand-mills, and cuts off grain to villages that disobey; the stolen secret lay in a chamber under the granary; "a hundred years".
- Customs shown (corn doll from the last sheaf, hand-binding at weddings, offerings at a standing stone) are real British and Irish folk practice, used for the Celtic colour.
